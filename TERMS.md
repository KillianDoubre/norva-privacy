## What Norva is

Norva grades packaged food on how it relates to five training outcomes —
endurance, power, strength, hypertrophy and recovery — for the sport and goal you
told it about. It explains every grade and cites the research each rule comes
from.

If you have a medical condition, an allergy, an eating disorder, or are pregnant,

## Allergens and dietary conflicts

If you tell Norva which foods you do not eat, it will warn you when a product
appears to conflict. **That warning is informational and cannot be relied upon
for safety.**

- It reads Open Food Facts data, which is contributed by volunteers and may be
  incomplete, out of date or wrong.
- When curated tags are missing it guesses from the ingredient text, and says so.
- **Silence is never approval.** No warning does not mean a product is safe,
  suitable or certified. Certification such as halal or kosher cannot be
  determined from an ingredient list at all.

**Always read the physical label.** If an allergy could harm you, the packaging is
the only source to trust.

## Grades are opinions, not measurements

A grade is the output of rules we wrote, applied to data someone else published.
Both can be wrong. The rules are open to inspection in _Method and sources_,
together with the research behind them, precisely so you can judge them yourself.

Nutrition science changes, and so will the grades. A product's grade may differ
between versions of the app.

## Product data

Product and nutrition data comes from [Open Food Facts](https://openfoodfacts.org)
under the Open Database License. We do not own it, verify it or guarantee it.
A product may be missing, mislabelled or carry the wrong nutrition values.
