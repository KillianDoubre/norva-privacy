# Privacy policy

**Norva** · last updated 29 July 2026

---

## The short version

Norva has no account, no server and no analytics. Everything you enter and
everything you scan stays on your phone. What leaves it goes to one place only —
Open Food Facts, to look food up: the barcode you scanned, the aisle you are
asking about, and the product photos the app displays.

## What stays on your phone

Stored in a database on your device and nowhere else:

- **Your profile** — first name if you gave one, sports, goals, sessions per
  week, body mass if you gave one, and any foods you said you don't eat.
- **Your scan history** — the products you scanned, when, the grade each was
  given, and whether you said you took it or put it back.
- **Cached product data** — nutrition facts fetched from Open Food Facts, kept so
  the app works offline and to avoid re-asking for the same product.
- **Your settings** — theme, language, and whether the Sunday reminder is on.

None of this is transmitted anywhere. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

## What leaves your phone

Every request below goes to [Open Food Facts](https://openfoodfacts.org) and
nowhere else. Each one carries your device's IP address, as any web request does,
so Open Food Facts can see what is being looked up from your connection. They are
a non-profit collaborative database and their own privacy policy governs what they
do with it.

**The barcode you scan.** When you scan a product, Norva asks for its nutrition
data. There is no other way to get the facts for a product you have just picked
up.

**The aisle, when the app looks for a better option.** To answer "better, in the
same aisle" it searches that product's category. That request carries the category
and **the country your phone's region setting reports** — without it the search
answers on the whole database and could name a product sold five thousand
kilometres away. It never carries your profile, your history or your grades.

**Product photos.** The app shows the picture Open Food Facts holds for a product,
on the product screen and in your history. Loading a picture is a request for that
picture, so opening your history asks for the photos of the products in it.

If you tap a scientific source in _Method and sources_, your browser opens
PubMed. That is a normal web visit, subject to PubMed's policy.

**Nothing else is sent, ever** — not your profile, not your scans, not your
grades, not your settings.

## What Norva does not do

- No account, no sign-in, no email address.
- No analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, no tracking of any kind.
- No profiling, no automated decision-making about you.
- Nothing is sold or shared, because nothing is collected.

## Subscriptions

Payment is handled entirely by the App Store or Google Play. Norva never sees your
card, your billing address or your store account. It receives one answer from the
store — whether this device holds an active subscription — and stores that answer
on the device.

## The camera

The camera is used to read barcodes and nothing else. No image is captured,
stored or transmitted. The frames are read for a barcode and discarded.

## Notifications

Optional and off by default. If you turn on the Sunday reminder, the notification
is scheduled by your phone, locally. No server is involved and nothing is sent to
us — there is no "us" to send to.

## Your data, your control

- **Export** — Profile → Your data → Export a backup writes a JSON file and hands
  it to your phone's share sheet. You choose where it goes; it is not uploaded
  anywhere on your behalf.
- **Delete** — press and hold a scan in your history to select scans and delete
  them, or clear the whole history at once. Uninstalling removes everything,
  permanently.
- **Share a verdict** — the card is drawn on your phone and saved to its temporary
  storage, then handed to the share sheet. Where it goes next is whatever app you
  pick, and that app's policy applies from there. The card deliberately carries no
  first name and no history.

There is no data to request from us, correct with us, or ask us to erase, because
we hold none. Under GDPR terms: we are not a data controller for anything you
enter, since it never reaches us.

## Health data

Norva explains how foods relate to training outcomes. It does not diagnose,
treat or prevent any condition, and does not replace advice from a doctor or a
registered dietitian. It is not a medical device.

## Children

Not directed at children under 13.

## Changes

If a future version starts collecting anything, this policy changes first and the
app says so before it does.
